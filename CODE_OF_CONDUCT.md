# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project draws other people's culture; that requires more care than an average
open-source repository, not less.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, including from people whose first language is not yours.
- Correct facts, not people. "This is a goje, not a banjo" is the most valuable kind of
  contribution here.
- Accept correction the same way. A misnamed icon is a bug, and the person reporting it is doing
  the project a service.
- Credit the people who confirm names and referents. That work is as real as the drawing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Dismissing a cultural correction because it came without a citation.
- Publishing a name, referent or claim you know to be unverified.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Email `icons@neustackstudio.com`. Reports are handled by the maintainers and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Where the behaviour concerns a maintainer,
escalate to the same address and expect it to be handled by someone else.
